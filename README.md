# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This HR Analytics Dashboard was built in Power BI to analyze workforce trends, employee distribution, attrition patterns, and departmental performance within an organizational environment.

The project was designed to transform raw HR data into actionable insights that support workforce planning, employee retention strategies, and management decision-making.


## Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to identify the underlying factors affecting employee retention, workforce distribution, and departmental performance due to fragmented or unstructured HR data.

This dashboard was created to answer important HR and business questions such as:

- Which departments experience the highest attrition?
- What workforce trends impact employee retention?
- How is employee distribution structured across departments?
- Which employee groups may require greater retention focus?
- What patterns can HR teams use to improve workforce planning?

## Objectives

- Analyze employee and departmental trends
- Identify attrition and retention patterns
- Visualize workforce distribution
- Support HR decision-making through interactive reporting
- Build an executive-style HR dashboard for business insights

## Tools & Technologies Used

## Key Insights

- Certain departments experienced noticeably higher attrition trends
- Workforce distribution patterns revealed staffing concentration across key areas
- Employee demographics highlighted potential retention focus groups
- Interactive filtering improved departmental and workforce analysis
- HR metrics provided clearer visibility into organizational workforce trends
